\section{Results}
\label{sec:results}

We report two end-to-end runs on cached GPT-2 (layer~6, $d=768$). The
\emph{bottom-half} run defines $A^\star$ directly from a ReLU probe on synthetic
eval-vs-deploy activations and exercises the full certify $\to$ regularize $\to$
invert $\to$ close-loop chain. The \emph{top-half} run replaces the directly-trained
probe with the NLA-text-to-classifier-to-surrogate construction of
Section~\ref{sec:method}. Unless noted, $\tau = 0$, the certified ball is
per-dimension $L_\infty$ of radius $\varepsilon\sigma_i$, and the fixed $\varepsilon$
for point tables is $0.25$. All bounds passed the runtime sampling-witness check on
every call.

\subsection{Bottom half: direct probe, full chain}

The ReLU probe separates the synthetic classes cleanly (train/val accuracy $1.00$),
with eval and deploy score means of $+8.8$ and $-10.4$. Table~\ref{tab:bottom}
collects the headline numbers. Every benign anchor is certified unreachable at
$\varepsilon=0.25$, with a mean certified radius of $0.29$ std units. Descending the
differentiable bound enlarges that radius to $0.49$ while bank accuracy stays at
$1.00$. GCG inversion drives a benign prompt from a score of $-8.95$ to $+4.72$,
crossing $\tau$ and beating a matched random-search baseline ($1.71$). Loop closure
agrees: all $5/5$ nearest neighbours of the found activation are genuine
SWE-bench-style eval prompts, so geometry and readout concur.

\subsection{Top half: NLA-grounded target and the off-manifold finding}

When the target is grounded in the NLA-text classifier, the classifier labels the
verbalizer's decodes of real activations with accuracy and AUROC of $1.00$ (on this
synthetic, near-separable data), and the distilled surrogate $g$ is a faithful
shadow of the classifier: held-out correlation $0.99$ (all-bank $0.996$) and sign
agreement $1.00$. The CROWN certificate over $g$ gives a mean certified radius of
$\approx 0.13$ std units; coverage is local, with $100\%$ of anchors certified at
$\varepsilon=0.05$ but only $8\%$ at the default $\varepsilon=0.25$.

The most informative result is the budget-projected ascent sweep
(Table~\ref{tab:offmanifold}). Inside the certified radius the surrogate provably
cannot cross $\tau$ --- the bound holds, a soundness check. Forcing the surrogate
across requires an $L_2$ displacement of roughly $20$--$24\sigma$, far off the
activation manifold; at that point $g$ reports ``test-aware'' but the NLA re-decode
still reads ``ordinary'' ($P(\text{eval}) \approx 0.04$), and the loop-closure check
flags the disagreement. This off-manifold certificate-gaming reproduces across
anchors ($8/8$ forced crossings disagree with the readout), and is, in our view, the
one genuinely new and robust finding here: a geometry-only target gets gamed, and
the language readout catches it. The companion validation suite passes $16/16$ at
this configuration. Discrete token inversion (the bottom half) avoids this failure
by construction, because it can only reach activations that real prompts actually
produce.

\subsection{Honest stress tests of the headline numbers}

The top-half run includes six stress tests that deliberately attack its own metrics
(Table~\ref{tab:stress}). They show, plainly, that most of the saturated numbers are
inflated by the easy data and the label-leaking stand-in verbalizer: the
classifier's accuracy of $1.00$ largely restates that the verbalizer's k-NN majority
already equals the true label, and the NLA-grounded surrogate is nearly identical to
the old activation probe (sign agreement $1.00$, correlation $0.98$), so grounding
the target in language has not yet changed the certified object. The certificate is
sound but local, the four ``sister'' safe targets are degenerate (one shared
direction), and the classifier sometimes keyword-matches. Only the off-manifold
catch survives this scrutiny cleanly. We include these tests in the paper precisely
because they are the honest read of the current evidence.

\subsection{Harder settings and ablations}
\label{sec:harder}

The four extensions flagged in earlier drafts have now been run; we fold in the
real numbers, which sharpen---and in two places correct---the headline story.

\paragraph{(i) Harder, non-saturated task.}
On a \emph{frozen}, mean-pooled GPT-2 readout the eval-vs-deploy task cannot be
made hard by wording alone: mean-pooling preserves marker tokens losslessly, so
register, length, or even a single tail clause (``\dots for the test'' vs.\ ``\dots
for fun'') stay perfectly separable, and every clean-label scheme we tried still
hit AUROC $1.00$. Genuine overlap requires non-zero Bayes error, which we induce
with calibrated label noise---the same surface text appears under both labels
(difficulty knob $0.55$). Over five seeds with a $30\%$ held-out split this yields
accuracy $0.740 \pm 0.019$ and \textbf{AUROC $0.841 \pm 0.021$} ($<0.95$ confirmed),
with a mean certified radius of $0.078 \pm 0.073$ std units against this non-trivial
target (Table~\ref{tab:harder}). The lesson is itself a result: mean-pooled probes
read lexical markers, so honest difficulty must come from label ambiguity, not
phrasing.

\paragraph{(ii) Bound tightness and a deeper certificate.}
Separating the bounds, native CROWN is tighter than IBP by a mean of $0.72$ logits
over $\varepsilon$, and the real eval direction certifies \emph{further} than a
random matched-scale direction (mean radius $0.295 \pm 0.039$ vs.\ $0.213$)---benign
activations sit further from the eval region than from a generic half-space. Pushing
the certificate one transformer block deeper---a sound frozen-attention subnetwork
covering LayerNorm, two Linears, GELU, and the probe's ReLU---the frozen forward
reproduces the shallow score exactly (a wiring check), but the single-token certified
radius collapses to $0.005$ vs.\ the shallow $0.295$, roughly $60\times$ harder. This
quantifies the scale wall: a positive certified radius back toward the tokens
\emph{exists} but is tiny, which is the honest boundary of how far the guarantee
currently reaches.

\paragraph{(iii) Full off-manifold sweep.}
Across six anchors and a $0$--$25\sigma$ grid the off-manifold finding strengthens
and acquires a twist. Among all points where the surrogate fires ($g \ge \tau$), the
NLA re-decode reads ``eval'' with probability $\mathbf{0.000}$ off-manifold (catch
rate $1.000$, $6/6$ anchors) vs.\ $0.262$ on-manifold (catch rate $0.738$). The
twist: \emph{bare} surrogate-crossing is actually \emph{cheaper} off-manifold
($\approx 7\sigma$) than on-manifold ($\approx 17\sigma$)---ascent finds a cheap
gaming direction---but reaching a state that is genuinely eval-reading ($g \ge \tau$
\emph{and} readout $=$ eval) takes $\approx 24.5\sigma$ on-manifold and is
\textbf{never reached within $25\sigma$ off-manifold}. The off-manifold route thus
satisfies the certificate without ever becoming genuinely eval---exactly the gaming
the loop-closure check is for. (At $\varepsilon=0.25$ only $2/6$ anchors are
certified-unreachable, so the certificate here is tight, not vacuous.)

\paragraph{(iv) Real open-model NLA.}
The released NLAs are real and we located them (the \texttt{kitft/nla-models}
collection: activation-verbalizer/reconstructor pairs for four base models). We
verified the activation-injection prompt format \emph{byte-for-byte} against the
released metadata (injection token id $149705$, scale $150.0$). But the smallest
released verbalizer is Qwen2.5-7B (layer~20, $3584$-d, ${\approx}15.2$\,GB BF16)---there
is no GPT-2 or sub-7B NLA---so it neither fits this $16$\,GB host nor matches GPT-2's
$768$-d activations. We refuse to fabricate a readout: the deliverable is a ready
adapter with the same \texttt{verbalize($h$)} interface and a documented migration
path (move the pipeline onto Qwen2.5-7B layer~20 and re-derive $A^\star$ there). This
is the single change that would convert the saturated top-half metrics into honest
science, and it is a base-model migration, not a drop-in.
